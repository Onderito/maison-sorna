# Tea leaf cutouts

Created using the built-in image generation tool in background-extraction edit mode.
Each supplied tea photograph was processed separately with a transparent background.

## Final assets

- `public/figma/infuser/product/pivoine-royale-feuilles.png`
- `public/figma/infuser/product/brume-des-cimes-feuilles.png`
- `public/figma/infuser/product/ecorce-de-feu-feuilles.png`
- `public/figma/infuser/product/miel-dorchidee-feuilles.png`
- `public/figma/infuser/product/sourcil-dor-feuilles.png`

## Prompt set

The following prompt was used once per asset, with `{tea}` replaced by its handle:
`pivoine-royale`, `brume-des-cimes`, `ecorce-de-feu`, `miel-dorchidee`, `sourcil-dor`.

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied photograph of {tea} tea leaves inside a white bowl. Extract ONLY the existing loose pile of dry tea leaves from this exact photo onto a genuinely transparent RGBA background. Remove the entire ceramic bowl, every white porcelain pixel, the bamboo/wood tray, table, all surroundings and all cast shadows from the bowl. Keep the real tea leaves, tiny stems and their internal shadows as photographed: same shapes, same colours, same arrangement, same viewing angle, natural rough edge and photographic texture. Do not invent, replace, restyle or add tea leaves. Preserve the distinct physical character of this particular tea. The result must be one isolated pile of these actual tea leaves, centered on a square transparent canvas with 15 percent transparent margin on all sides. No container, no surface, no background, no backdrop, no text, no label, no gradient, no decorative elements, no drop shadow, no white halo. Transparent gaps between leaves where the bowl would show through. This is a faithful product cutout, not a new product illustration.

The source photographs remain unchanged. Image generation may reconstruct fine leaf details; these outputs are not pixel-exact masks of the originals.
